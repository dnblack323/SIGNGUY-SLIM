# Step 3A Expenses and Sales Tax

Step 3A adds bounded finance tracking to SignGuy Slim. It is not a full accounting system, tax filing system, bank integration, inventory system, Stripe integration, Price Lab integration, Webstore integration, Artwork Approval workflow, onboarding expansion, Release E document polish, or Facebook/Meta Stage 9 work.

## Scope

Step 3A includes:

- Expense records with expense date, vendor, category, description, amount, payment method, archived state, audit history, and one optional receipt attachment.
- Basic expense filtering by date range, category, payment method, archive state, and text search.
- Expense summaries for selected periods, month-to-date, year-to-date, category totals, and payment-method totals.
- Sales-tax reporting from finalized invoice snapshots.
- Monthly, quarterly, yearly, and custom sales-tax period selectors.
- Sales-tax summary values for taxable sales, non-taxable sales, tax collected, gross sales, and included document count.
- A concise sales-tax limitation notice in the product UI.

## Non-Goals

Step 3A does not include:

- Tax filing, remittance, jurisdiction rules, tax advice, or tax agency integrations.
- Bank, card, Plaid, accounting-package, or credentialed payment-provider imports.
- Expense OCR, camera capture, annotation, receipt matching automation, or inventory costing.
- Customer-document redesign, quote or invoice PDF polish, Stripe payments, Webstores, Artwork Approval, Price Lab calculators, or Price Lab shell integration.
- Stage 9 Facebook/Meta code, configuration, dependencies, navigation, placeholders, webhooks, OAuth, or feature flags.

## Data Ownership

Expense records are tenant business data. The tenant owns the commercial bookkeeping fact: when money was spent, who the vendor was, what category it belongs to, the amount in integer cents, and the optional receipt attachment metadata.

Expense receipt files are private tenant files. Their stored bytes are managed through the same durable attachment root and validation posture used by existing private order attachments, but they are linked to expense records rather than Orders.

Sales-tax reporting does not create a new tax ledger table in Step 3A. It derives reports from existing finalized invoice snapshots so historical tax amounts remain tied to the commercial document the shop actually issued.

## Authorization

Owner, Admin, and Manager roles can create, view, edit, archive, attach, download, and remove expense receipts.

Owner and Admin roles can view sales-tax reports. Managers do not receive the company sales-tax report in Step 3A because the current commercial-readiness boundary keeps tax reporting in the owner/admin financial lane. Staff users cannot manage expenses or view company sales-tax reporting.

Backend authorization is authoritative. Frontend navigation only reflects the backend rules and is not relied on as a security boundary.

## Source-of-Truth Rules

Expense amount is stored as integer cents. Date-only fields use `YYYY-MM-DD`. Archive state is non-destructive; archived expenses are excluded from normal summaries unless explicitly requested.

Sales-tax reporting uses invoices as the included-document source. Quotes, Orders, and Payments are not counted directly, which prevents the same sale from being counted multiple times.

Included invoices are finalized commercial documents:

- `issued` invoices are included.
- `draft` invoices are excluded.
- `void` invoices are excluded.

Tax collected is the invoice `tax_cents` snapshot. Gross sales is the invoice `total_cents` snapshot. Taxable and non-taxable sales are derived from the invoice's linked Order item snapshots, using each Order item's historical `taxable` flag and `line_total_cents`. If an issued invoice has no recoverable item split, the report keeps invoice totals authoritative and treats the split as unknown rather than inventing a tax basis.

Payments are intentionally not the source for tax collected. Slim tracks manual payment status separately from issued invoice tax snapshots.

## Expense Attachments

Each expense can have one optional receipt attachment. Receipt uploads use the existing private upload validation posture:

- allowed MIME types only;
- blocked executable/script-like extensions rejected;
- maximum upload size enforced;
- content sniffing applied for supported file types;
- SHA-256 recorded for stored bytes;
- tenant-scoped storage keys generated by the server;
- no public filesystem paths or predictable public URLs.

Removing an expense receipt clears the expense's attachment metadata and deletes the private stored receipt file through the durable file helper. Archiving an expense preserves the record and its current receipt attachment unless the user removes the receipt separately.

## Backup and Portability

Expense records and expense receipt files are tenant business data and are included in Slim's encrypted customer portable backup after Step 3A. Runtime authentication/session state remains excluded.

Server infrastructure backups remain separate from customer portable backups. Step 3A does not replace customer portable backups with raw SQLite backups and does not require server-backup metadata for future Slim-to-MVP portability.

The shared portability repository remains unchanged in Step 3A unless a later explicitly authorized cross-repository contract update is required.
